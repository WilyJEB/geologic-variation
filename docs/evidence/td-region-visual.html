What was computed, what was invented

Reused observations: seed 8675309, native query [z10880,x2688,z13953,x5761), 3,073² = 9,443,329 originally queried pixels. Only 49×49 elevations at 64-pixel spacing remain here; 9,440,928 query values and all exterior heights are unavailable. The original full-patch hash is provenance, not a reconstructed array.

New descriptors: sampled height differences and, for each row, the x centroid weighted by elevation above that row’s minimum. This is not a ridge axis or relief error estimate. Invented rules: 2,048-pixel lattice, hash phases/history labels, half-gain ±256 contact displacement, four-row edge taper and linear contact interpolation. Horizontal contacts stay fixed. There is no contact selection or geological confidence threshold.

Whole conceptual cells are connected by an invertible shear; the table checks four-neighbour components of actual labelled sites separately. That does not prove a player will recognize these regions in-game. No blocks, palettes or saved worlds changed.

Finite support, identity and remaining gates

At most two rows (98 retained heights) supply a displacement, five observed points supply a difference and nine IDs form the fixed regional neighborhood. Mathematical floor handles negative coordinates; half-open boundaries choose the larger integer cell at an exact contact. SHA256 domain separation fixes phases and history tokens. Surface and depth share one history token.
